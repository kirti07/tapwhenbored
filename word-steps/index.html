What is this?

Word Steps is a free daily word ladder puzzle. Change one letter at a time to turn the start word into the target word — every word in between has to be a real word. There's one puzzle a day, unlimited attempts, and your best step count is saved so you can keep trying to beat it. No score, no timer, no signup.

How to play

  • Tap a letter in the current word, then pick its replacement.
  • If that's a real word, it locks in as your next step automatically.
  • Reach the target in as few steps as possible.
  • Already solved it? Come back and try for fewer steps — your best today is saved.
  • A new puzzle unlocks every day at midnight.
