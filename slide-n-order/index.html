How to play

  • Tap a tile next to the empty slot to slide it in.
  • Arrange every number, 1 through 15, in order.
  • No timer — take as long as you like.
  • Tiles glow softly once they land in the right spot.
  • Goal: all 15 tiles in order, empty slot last.
