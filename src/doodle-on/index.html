What is this?

Doodle On is a free online drawing game with one rule: don't lift your finger. The moment you lift, the game ends and whatever you drew is your result. No score, no timer, no signup — just see how far one continuous line can take you.

How to play

  • Touch the canvas and start drawing.
  • Keep your finger down — lifting ends the game.
  • Draw anywhere, cross your own line as much as you like.
  • No score, no timer — just see how far you can go.
