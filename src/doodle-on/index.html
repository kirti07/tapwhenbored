What is this?

Doodle On is a free online drawing game. Each round hands you a starting shape and an unrelated idea — a spiral, and "something that flies" — plus 30 seconds to turn one into the other. Pencil, paint bucket, six colours, one-step undo. No score, no signup. Share the result as a picture.

How to play

  • Every round gives you a shape and a direction.
  • You get 30 seconds to turn one into the other. The clock starts on your first mark.
  • Pencil to draw, bucket to fill a closed area, tap a colour to change it.
  • Undo takes back the last thing you did. Hit Done when you're happy, then share it.
