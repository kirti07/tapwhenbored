What is this?

Honeycomb is a free online puzzle: clear every bomb from an irregular hex hive without breaking it apart. Tap any hidden tile — a bomb explodes and leaves a gap, and a safe tile shifts one place clockwise before showing how many bombs sit around it, or stays put and shows the count where it is if that place is taken. Numbered tiles become fixed landmarks. Every hive is randomly generated and always clearable. No signup, no download.

How to play

  • Clear every bomb without breaking the hive into pieces.
  • Tap any ? tile — some are safe, some are bombs. Every hidden tile can be tapped, wherever it sits in the hive.
  • Safe: it shifts one place clockwise, then shows how many bombs touch its new cell. If that cell is taken, it stays and shows the count where it is.
  • Bomb: it explodes and leaves an empty hex.
  • Numbered tiles are landmarks. They never move again.
  • The run ends if the hive comes apart. Clear every bomb and you win.
