What is this?

Marble Nostalgia is a free online version of the classic peg-solitaire marble board (also known as Brainvita). Jump one marble over another into an empty hole to capture it — the goal is to finish with a single marble left. Undo any move, no signup, no download, just a quiet board to work through whenever you're bored.

How to play

  • Tap a marble to select it.
  • Glowing holes show where it can jump.
  • Tap a glowing hole to jump — the marble in between is captured.
  • No mistakes: use undo ↶ anytime.
  • Goal: finish with one marble.
