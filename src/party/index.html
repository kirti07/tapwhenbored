                What is Tap Party?

                Tap Party is a free party game for 2 to 12 people in the same room, played on everyone's own phone. One person starts a party and picks the game; everyone else scans the QR code or types the four-letter room code and adds a name and a face. In Flip It, the lights-out puzzle, and Slide N Order, the 15-puzzle, the room races the same board at the same moment against a time limit the host picks. In Doodle On everyone turns the same shape into the same idea, then votes, anonymously, for the best one. In Humour Me everyone finishes the same phrase and the room votes for the funniest. In Sounds Sus everyone knows a secret word except the spy, and the room votes players out until the spy is caught. The results reveal together on every phone. Go again with the same group in one tap, or switch games. No app, no account, no signup.
