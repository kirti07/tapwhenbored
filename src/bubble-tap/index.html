What is this?

Tap When Bored is a free online bubble wrap popping game for whenever you're bored. It's the same oddly satisfying burst as popping a pimple or real bubble wrap — pure nostalgic time-pass, no mess, no plastic wasted. Tap bubbles to score points, but some hide a bomb — pop one and it's game over. No download, no signup, just endless bubbles whenever boredom hits.

How to play

  • Tap bubbles to pop them. Every pop scores.
  • Pop several quickly for a combo — the streak multiplies your score.
  • Some bubbles hide a bomb. Pop one and the run ends.
  • A bubble's colour is a hint once you have tapped it: purple is safe, yellow is unstable.
  • Pause any time, and use settings to turn off sound or motion.
