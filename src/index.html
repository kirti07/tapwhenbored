About

Questions

Tap When Bored is a small shelf of free, single-player browser games for whenever you have a couple of minutes to fill — a queue, a commute, a break between two things. Every game opens straight in the browser with no signup, no download and no account, and each one is built to be understood in a few seconds and played with one thumb.

Are the games on Tap When Bored free?

Yes. Every game is free to play, with no in-app purchases.

Do I need to sign up or download anything?

No. There's no signup and nothing to install — each game opens straight in your browser.

Do the games work on mobile?

Yes. Every game is built for touch and works on phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

More tiny games coming soon. No account needed. Ever.
