What is this?

Slide N Order is a free online 15-puzzle — the classic sliding tile game. Arrange numbered tiles 1 through 15 back into order around a single empty slot, one slide at a time. No timer, no signup, no download, just moves — take as long as you like.

How to play

  • Tap a tile next to the empty slot to slide it in.
  • Only tiles touching the empty slot can move.
  • Work the numbers back into order, 1 to 15, reading left to right.
  • Every slide counts as a move — fewer is better.
  • No timer. Take as long as you like, and your best is saved.
