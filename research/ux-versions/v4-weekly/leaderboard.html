How this board works
  • The weekly table empties every Sunday at 00:00 in your own timezone. Last week stays readable for seven days.
  • Flip It ranks on moves, fewest first, and only counts runs on Medium, so every row is the same puzzle size.
  • Same score, same rank number. Ties go to whoever got there first.
  • Par is the maker’s own target for this game — 10 moves. It is seeded so the board is never empty, and it never wins the week.
  • Scores are sent from your browser and we can’t verify them. Play honestly; it’s a bubble-wrap site.
